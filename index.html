What to check before ordering

  1. Confirm the exact variant. Open the CAFANS product page and follow its source listing. Check the selected size and color, then compare the seller’s measurements with a similar item you own.
  2. Review quality-control photos. If your agent offers warehouse photos, check the size label, color and stitching. Ask for measurements if fit is unclear and check the return deadline.
  3. Compare the delivered cost. Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent and payment fees, international shipping, and any applicable tax or duty. Ask which shipping lines charge by volumetric weight.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping research list for comparing product links, photos and reference prices. This independent page uses CAFANS as its catalog source.

Can I use these listings with ACBuy?

Open the product page to find the original marketplace listing. Check whether ACBuy currently accepts that marketplace and item before adding it to an order.

Are the product images warehouse QC photos?

These are listing photos. Ask your agent for warehouse photos of the specific item and variant you ordered if that service is available.
